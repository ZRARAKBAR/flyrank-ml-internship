## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this capstone is to establish a repeatable process for expanding my personal portfolio with real projects rather than treating my portfolio as a one-time website.

For every future project, I will turn the work into a concise case study that explains the problem, what I personally did, and what resulted from the work.

This gives future projects a consistent structure and makes my portfolio easier for universities, employers, and other reviewers to understand.

# 3. Three-Beat Case Study Structure

I will reuse the three-beat structure introduced earlier in the General AI Fluency track.

## Beat 1 — Problem

First, I will explain:

* What problem existed?
* Who experienced the problem?
* Why did the problem matter?
* What was the goal of the project?

This section will be short and understandable to someone who does not have a technical background.

## Beat 2 — What I Did

Next, I will explain what I actually built and contributed.

This section will cover:

* My responsibilities
* Technologies and tools used
* Main implementation steps
* Important design decisions
* AI tools used during development
* What I personally checked, tested, or changed

I will distinguish between AI-assisted work and decisions or validation that I performed myself.

## Beat 3 — What Came of It

Finally, I will explain the result.

This section will include:

* What was successfully built
* What the final system can do
* Evaluation or testing results where available
* What I learned
* Current limitations
* What I would improve in a future version

This structure will keep future case studies focused on evidence rather than simply listing technologies.

---

# 4. Process for Adding a Future Case Study

Whenever I complete a significant project, I will follow this process:

### Step 1 — Identify the project

Choose a completed project that demonstrates a meaningful technical or problem-solving outcome.

### Step 2 — Define the problem

Write a short explanation of the problem, target users, and objective.

### Step 3 — Document my contribution

Record what I personally designed, implemented, tested, and improved.

### Step 4 — Record the result

Document the final functionality, evaluation results, lessons learned, and limitations.

### Step 5 — Create the case study

Convert the information into the three-beat format:

**Problem → What I Did → What Came of It**

### Step 6 — Add supporting evidence

Add relevant screenshots, repository links, live-demo links, evaluation results, or other evidence.

### Step 7 — Publish

Add the case study to the Projects / Case Studies section of my personal website.

### Step 8 — Review

Before publishing, I will check that the case study is:

* Specific
* Accurate
* Understandable
* Honest about limitations
* Clear about my own contribution
* Transparent about AI assistance
